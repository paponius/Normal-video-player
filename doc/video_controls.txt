Keyboard will control:
First video that starts playing. (itself by autoplay, or by pushing a play button)
When another video starts playing in parallel with previous one, and is un-muted, it will become the controlled one.

Play button
Play button can be the same as pause button. In such case, when a video is targeted to play and another one is still playing,
this common play/pause button will both pause the previous video and start playing the new one.
The most visible video on screen, or the first one if multiple are completely visible will play on play button push.

How media elements are found
On page load, the page is scanned for media elements including all in Shadow DOM.
This will not find elements lazy loaded, or loaded interactively,
such will be found when they start playing (by playing listener) if they are not in Shadow DOM,
or when they are clicked also in Shadow DOM.
